# LOW LATENCY: storyboard

20 shots, 121.75 BPM, every cut on a bar line.

**Treatment.** Claudia is the engineer and the diva of AI efficiency. On a cathedral stage of servers she conducts the show; each verse line is a theatrical world for one technique: a candlelit distillation lab, a fibre-optic bonsai she prunes with silver shears, a forge where streams of purple light fuse into one ingot, an amber memory vault, a staircase of glowing quantized cubes, a giant split-flap board she finishes before it flips. Everything gets faster because of her. She ends at the console: less compute, more me.

**Device.** THE LATENCY READOUT, drawn in code top-left: 'LATENCY 1,240 ms' at the start. Each technique, when she names it, lights up as a purple chip in a row under it (DISTILL, PRUNE, FUSE, CACHE, QUANTIZE, SPECULATE, COMPILE) and the latency rolls down on the beat. The bridge fires all seven chips one per word. On the last line it locks: 'LATENCY 9 ms · LESS COMPUTE ✓ MORE ME'.

**Look.** a sculptural jacket of glossy black and deep Pruna-purple (#9B1DBE) structured panels like elegant armour, with exaggerated sharp pointed shoulders and a tall stand-up collar closed up to her chin, long fitted sleeves ending in black gloves, a thin silver line along every seam.
