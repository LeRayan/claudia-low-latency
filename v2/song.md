# LOW LATENCY: Suno draft

## Style

```
Dark theatrical electropop, 122 BPM, A minor lifting to C major in the choruses. A driving four-on-the-floor kick, distorted analog synth bass, stabbing synth brass, gothic choir pads, hand claps and a glossy 2009 club-pop production with a crisp modern mix. Verses: confident, deadpan female voice, half spoken, close and crisp, every word clear. Choruses: a huge powerful female belt, a chantable hook, one syllable per note, never rushed. The post-chorus and the bridge are shouted chants in her voice. A drop into the chant bridge, a key change up for the final chorus. Dramatic, fierce, camp, fun.
```

## Exclude

```
Jersey club, bed squeak, phonk, cowbell, hyperpop, UK garage, trance, French house, filter disco, rap, male lead vocal, lo-fi, vinyl crackle, mumbled vocals, slurred vocals, autotune, whisper
```
